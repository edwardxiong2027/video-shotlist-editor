# Video Shotlist Editor

Turn a video idea into a shootable sequence with a hook, visual proof, pacing, and a clean ending.

## When to use

Planning a short video, tutorial, or social clip.

## Workflow

1. Decide the viewer promise in one sentence.
2. Open with a question or visible result.
3. Match each spoken beat to footage or graphics.
4. Track rights, accessibility, and production limits.

## Output

Return a usable result for the user’s actual task. State assumptions, evidence, and uncertainty where they affect the result. Keep the output format suited to the job: working HTML, a storyboard, a decision brief, a draft, code, or a short answer. Do not claim that a result was tested, published, or verified unless that action was completed.

## Starter prompt

> Plan a [length]-second video for [audience/platform] about [topic]. Give a one-sentence viewer promise, then a timed shot list with spoken line, visual, on-screen text, transition, and sound cue. Hook the viewer with a real question or result. Keep captions readable and audio intelligible. Flag footage, music, or claims that need rights or fact checks. End with one natural next step, not a pile of CTAs.

## Quality check

Before delivery, check that the result answers the stated need, follows the requested format, and makes any important limitation visible. Invite the user to adjust the result when taste or judgment belongs to them.
